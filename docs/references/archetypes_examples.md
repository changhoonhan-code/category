# Pacing Archetype Examples — Reference

Full DIRECTOR'S NOTES + TRANSCRIPT examples for all 7 archetypes.
Load this file when you need a delivery model for a specific archetype.

For archetype definitions, distribution guide, and when-to-use rules → `SKILL.md §5`.

> **These examples are ARCHETYPE CHARACTER DEMONSTRATIONS — not templates.**
> They show delivery character and TRANSCRIPT rhythm. DIRECTOR'S NOTES are
> at archetype-character level intentionally. For the full word-level depth
> expected in actual prompts, see the **Depth Calibration example** in
> `directing_reference.md`.

> **Based on a single reference product (Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer).**
> Product names, data, and features must never be carried over to other products.

## Archetype A — Staccato Cold Open

**When**: First block, `bgm_mood: Silence`. Voice carries everything alone.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: A
- Delivery: Near-suspended, low-pitched. Hard stops between every clause.
  Voice carries all weight alone — no music.
- Tone: Zero warmup. Staccato rhythm. Each sentence is a verdict.
- Key moments: Drop pitch on "Gone." Emphasize 'single' with higher pitch.
  Final sentence returns to confident authority.
- Emotional arc: Quiet, measured curiosity — each fragment lands as its own
  verdict. The intrigue comes from pauses and fragments, not from tags.

#### TRANSCRIPT
Ten percent of the battery.
[short pause]
Gone.
[short pause]
In three minutes.
Not from a phone call. Not from streaming music.
[short pause]
From a single three-minute video clip.
[medium pause]
This is what seven hundred sixty-eight reviews of the Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer actually say.
```

## Archetype B — Analytical Inventory → Reveal

**When**: Distribution data, enumerable stats + one outlier. Max once per 3-block stretch.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: B
- Delivery: Brisk, clinical scaffolding, then sudden half-speed brake on
  the outlier. Sharp consonants on "five hundred ninety-eight" and "fourteen percent".
- Tone: Reading a distribution chart as evidence. Sprint through the
  majority, hard brake on the anomaly.
- Context: "Whether the data confirms that promise is what this investigation is about."
- Key moments: Rapid-fire five-star through two-star as scaffolding.
  Hard brake on five hundred ninety-eight one-star — this is the reveal.
  "fourteen percent" gets full weight. Chew each digit with deliberate separation.
- Emotional arc: Open with deliberate gravity — the analyst laying context
  before the reveal. Shift to clinical scaffolding, hard brake into solemn weight.

#### TRANSCRIPT
Across its full review history, the Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer holds four point one stars — forty-two seventy-five total ratings.
The distribution has a specific shape.
[extremely fast]
Five-star reviews account for sixty-seven percent of all ratings — twenty-eight sixty-six buyers. Four-star: four twenty-seven. Three-star: two fifty-six. Two-star: one twenty-eight.
[long pause]
[speaking slowly]
And five hundred ninety-eight one-star reviews — a fourteen percent share.
That fourteen percent is not the ceiling. It's the baseline.
The seventy-nine-day window shows where it's heading.
```

## Archetype C — Sustained Gravity

**When**: Multi-paragraph dark data, cumulative weight. `bgm_mood: Dark` or `Tense`. No single outlier.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: C
- Delivery: Low-pitched, steady, unemotional. Weight on every syllable.
  Gravely matter-of-fact — the accumulation does the work.
- Tone: Clinical enumeration. Not dramatic — not mocking. Structural data exposure.
- Context: "But 8 positive reports against 38 negative in a 46-mention sample is a significant gap."
- Key moments: "zero positive" is the verdict — isolate it with full weight.
  "nine hundred twenty-eight buyers" gets [speaking slowly] — highest helpful-vote count is the thesis.
  Chew each digit of "nine hundred twenty-eight" with deliberate separation.
  Volume taper on the closing — let the finding close itself.

#### TRANSCRIPT
[speaking slowly]
The connectivity layer compounds the audio issues.
[short pause]
[speaking slowly]
Twenty-two Bluetooth-related reviews in the dataset — zero positive.
Sixteen software connectivity reviews — also zero positive.
The Bluetooth architecture is single-device: these glasses pair to one device at a time with no multi-point support.
Switching from an iPhone to an iPad requires a full re-pairing sequence every time.
Firmware updates introduce a separate failure vector: a mandatory update severed the WiFi connection used for photo transfer.
The glasses stopped connecting to the app entirely.
The documented workaround: delete the app, reinstall it, re-authenticate the device from scratch.
[medium pause]
[speaking slowly]
nine hundred twenty-eight buyers marked the review documenting these Bluetooth and software failures as helpful — the highest helpful-vote count of any review in the dataset.
That number tells you how many buyers ran into the same wall.
```

## Archetype D — Controlled Acceleration + Hard Landing

**When**: Buildup resolving in a single verdict sentence. `bgm_mood: Tense`.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: D
- Delivery: Measured start, building momentum, hard brake on final number.
  The collapse is a category change, not a degradation.
- Tone: Gravitas Drop register. Constraints stack, then the verdict lands.
- Context: "That's not a fringe finding."
- Key moments: "approximately twenty minutes" is the collapse — hard brake here.
  "That's not a variance. That's a different device." is a reframe — tempo
  drop entering the second sentence. Volume taper on "backup battery" —
  let the irony speak for itself.
- Emotional arc: Deliberate, measured opening — constraints stack with building
  momentum, then collapse into the cold verdict.

#### TRANSCRIPT
Disabling the assistant is a workaround, not a solution.
Cold weather removes it entirely.
In sub-freezing conditions, the three-to-four-hour battery window — the device's upper limit under normal use — collapses to
[speaking slowly]
approximately twenty minutes during video capture.
[medium pause]
For buyers using these primarily for outdoor recording, that's not a variance. That's a different device.
[extremely fast]
For users attempting to wear them throughout the day for mixed tasks, the reported reality is five to six full charging cycles.
Each cycle requires placing the glasses in the case.
The case requires external power.
Direct charging of the glasses is not possible — the case is the only charging path.
[short pause]
So your backup battery needs its own backup battery.
```

## Archetype E — Reflective Discovery

**When**: Inference blocks, root-cause analysis, curiosity loops.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: E
- Delivery: Deliberate, thoughtful, rising intrigue. Pause-before-insight rhythm.
  "I found the answer and I'm going to show you." Forward-leaning.
- Tone: Rising intrigue — the analyst reflecting on what they found.
- Context: "Something changed."
- Key moments: "Three patterns" builds momentum through the list.
  "Those three patterns explain the drop" is the thesis — long pause before it.
  Final sentence is warm and inviting — welcome the viewer in.
- Emotional arc: Open with deliberate introspection, shift to rising curiosity
  on the three patterns, settle into warm invitation at the close.

#### TRANSCRIPT
I analyzed seven hundred sixty-eight detailed reviews.
Three patterns kept appearing: a hardware-level power crisis, a physical comfort failure with a documented timeline, and a lens specification that catches nearly every new buyer off guard.
[long pause]
[speaking slowly]
Those three patterns explain the drop.
I'll show you all three — with the numbers and the buyer quotes that prove them.
```

## Archetype F — Warm Conversational

**When**: Hook praise baseline, positive context. `bgm_mood: Bright` or `Neutral`.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: F
- Delivery: Warm, slightly elevated pitch, flowing and natural.
  Friendly expert genuinely interested in the product.
- Tone: Curiosity present; hype absent. Conversational, not performative.
- Context: "I'll show you all three — with the numbers and the buyer quotes that prove them."
- Key moments: Feature list flows naturally — mild enthusiasm, not hype.
  "No screen. No heads-up display." uses quick staccato — short clauses
  landing cleanly one after another.
- Emotional arc: Warm confidence throughout — softer register on the opening,
  mild contained enthusiasm on features, quick staccato on the no-screen reveal.

#### TRANSCRIPT
The Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer sits at the intersection of fashion and wearable technology.
You get a twelve-megapixel point-of-view camera, open-ear audio speakers, and Meta AI voice access — all built into the classic Wayfarer frame.
The pitch is straightforward: wear a pair of glasses you'd wear anyway, and have hands-free capture and audio available on demand.
[short pause]
No screen. No heads-up display. Just a camera, speakers, and an AI you activate with your voice.
```

## Archetype G — Declarative Authority (Verdict)

**When**: Verdict blocks. Flat, even, certain. Conviction through steadiness.

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: G
- Delivery: Firm, even, confident. Verdict-like register.
  Trusted-advisor warmth. Conviction through steadiness.
- Tone: Vocal Smile on Buy If conditions. Firm, not enthusiastic.
  Each condition gets identical vocal weight — no ranking.
- Context: "The buyer shared them for a reason."
- Key moments: Opening sentence is the headline landing — slight tempo drop.
  "Buy this if" conditions all get identical, even pace.
  "The form factor is genuinely unmatched" is firm certainty — not celebration.
- Emotional arc: Solemn opening thesis, then shift to warmer advisory register
  on the conditions. Close with quiet certainty on the form factor appraisal.

#### TRANSCRIPT
[speaking slowly]
Seven hundred sixty-eight reviews distilled into one sentence: the Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarer is a capable casual capture device that rewards low-demand users and punishes everyone else.
[short pause]
Buy this if you fit a specific profile.
You want hands-free, first-person video clips — three minutes or less, casual social content, outdoor daylight conditions where the transition lenses actually activate.
You're primarily in the US, where the Meta AI features function as described.
You're comfortable treating these as a device you rotate rather than wear continuously — charging mid-day is a given, not an exception.
The camera will deliver for that use case.
The form factor is genuinely unmatched: no other smart glasses blend this cleanly into a classic frame.
```

## Hook Stage 1 — Universal Experience Example

**Context**: Robot vacuum battery failure video. `video_question`: "Why do 82 people's vacuum cleaners die at the exact same spot?"

```text
### DIRECTOR'S NOTES
- Archetype: E
- Delivery: Measured, low-pitched curiosity. Building intrigue slowly.
  This is the very first thing the viewer hears — no product context yet.
- Tone: Quiet curiosity. Plant a universally relatable scenario that hooks
  viewers regardless of product interest.
- Key moments: "Instead" gets the weight — tempo slows on this word.
  "Sound familiar?" — near-whisper, conspiratorial intimacy. The hook closes.
- Emotional arc: Measured curiosity planting a universally relatable scenario.
  Build to near-whisper on the final question — conspiratorial intimacy.

#### TRANSCRIPT
You've left the house.
[short pause]
You come back expecting a clean floor.
[short pause]
[speaking slowly]
Instead — a cold machine, frozen in the middle of the room.
[medium pause]
Sound familiar?
```
